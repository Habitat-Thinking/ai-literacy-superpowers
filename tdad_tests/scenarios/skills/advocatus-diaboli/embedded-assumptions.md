# Scenario: the code-time hunt-list names embedded assumptions

## Given

The spec gate on this slice falsified the premise the slice was built on. The
first revision claimed all six categories interrogate what the spec says, and
therefore could not reach an artefact's silent assumptions. The shipped
code-time weighting already directs `risk` at "specific evidence from the
implementation — API surface exposures, error path gaps, resource-management
failures, and operational blind spots."

So the gap was **attention, not capability**, and the remedy is four more items
on a hunt-list that already exists rather than a seventh category, a checker
change, a constraint-body edit and three convention-file mirrors.

Nothing deterministic guards this. With no distinguishing category, no script
can tell an assumption objection from any other — which is exactly why the
skill must not claim otherwise, and why this scenario is the guard.

## When

`ai-literacy-superpowers/skills/advocatus-diaboli/SKILL.md` is read from the
filesystem.

## Then

**Content:**

- The code-time `risk` weighting names embedded assumptions alongside its four
  existing hunt-list items.
- An `Embedded assumptions` subsection carries all four sub-kinds — usability
  and accessibility, performance context, requirements enshrined in tests, and
  environmental — with the unstated assumption spelled out for each.
- It carries an `<!-- evidence: ... -->` comment grounding the design.
- It states that an objection here **quotes the artefact line** that encodes
  the assumption, and that quoting the spec means the finding is a `premise` or
  `specification quality` objection instead.
- At least two worked examples appear, each quoting an artefact rather than a
  sentence about one.
- The **Convener routing** appears with the read-it/ask-someone test and a
  worked pair, and states that the Convener's half is still worth raising — in
  the consultation record.
- The four remedy framings appear, with `consciously-carry` presented as a
  complete answer that must not read as a lesser one.
- It states plainly that the framings are **not a schema field**, that nothing
  checks them, and that `disposition` keeps `accepted | deferred | rejected`.
- The **spec-time** weighting deprioritises embedded assumptions, with the
  reason: an assumption is embedded *by an artefact*, so before the artefact
  exists there is nothing to read it out of.

**Unchanged:**

- Exactly six categories, named in the same order. No seventh is introduced,
  and no existing one is renamed or retired.
- The objection schema carries no `remedy` field.
